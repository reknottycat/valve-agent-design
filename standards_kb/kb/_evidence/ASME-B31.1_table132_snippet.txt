[15377] GENERAL NOTES: 
[15378] 
[15379] (a) PWHT of P-No. 1 materials is not mandatory, provided that all of the following conditions are met: 
[15380] 
[15381] (1) the nominal thickness, as defined in para, 132.4.1, is % in. (19.0 mm) or less 
[15382] 
[15383] (2) a minimum preheat of 200°F (95°C) is applied when the nominal material thickness of either of the base metals exceeds 1 in. 
[15384] (25.0 mm) 
[15385] 
[15386] (b) PWHT of low hardenability P-No. 1 materials with a nominal material thickness, as defined in para. 132.4.3, over % in. (19.0 mm) but 
[15387] not more than lV 2 in. (38 mm) is not mandatory, provided all of the following conditions are met: 
[15388] 
[15389] (1) the carbon equivalent, CE, is < 0.50, using the formula 
[15390] 
[15391] CE = C + (Mn + Si)/6 + (Cr + Mo + V)/5 + (Ni + Cu)/15 
[15392] 
[15393] The maximum chemical composition limit from the material specification or actual values from a chemical analysis or material test 
[15394] report shall be used in computing CE. If analysis for the last two terms is not available, 0.1% may be substituted for those two terms 
[15395] as follows: 
[15396] 
[15397] CE = C + (Mn + Si)/6 + 0.1 
[15398] 
[15399] (2) a minimum preheat of 250°F (121°C) is applied 
[15400] 
[15401] (3) the maximum weld deposit thickness of each weld pass shall not exceed % in. (6 mm) 
[15402]
